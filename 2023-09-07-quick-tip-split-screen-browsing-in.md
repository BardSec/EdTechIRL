# Quick Tip: Split Screen Browsing in Edge

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!VwtE!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff423e501-07b9-4094-b4d5-a691dde4d711_1507x1448.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!VwtE!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff423e501-07b9-4094-b4d5-a691dde4d711_1507x1448.png)

If you ever find yourself in need of having two of your tabs open on the screen at the same time, hitting the split screen button in Edge is a quick solution:

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!JO1y!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8796e92c-e1b9-4a31-85b9-6c8274684d4b_1356x176.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!JO1y!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8796e92c-e1b9-4a31-85b9-6c8274684d4b_1356x176.png)

The most common time I benefit from this is while watching a video or viewing instructions in one pane, while following along in the other. It’s also useful for watching a video while taking notes.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!rM4k!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7b05f946-b1b3-4629-9bb8-582397acb1b8_2494x1226.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!rM4k!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7b05f946-b1b3-4629-9bb8-582397acb1b8_2494x1226.png)

Even though it’s possible to do this by opening two different windows, using the split screen button in Edge makes the task quick and snappy.
