Are you a high school teacher interested in empowering the next generation to navigate today’s digital landscape securely? The National Cybersecurity Teaching Academy (NCTA) offers an incredible opportunity to gain specialized skills in cybersecurity education and bring this critical knowledge into your classroom.

NCTA is a graduate certificate program designed specifically for high school teachers. Through online summer courses, you’ll dive into key topics like cybersecurity fundamentals, network security, and advanced cybersecurity concepts. This program prepares you to confidently teach high school cybersecurity courses, enhancing both your professional expertise and your students’ future readiness. Depending on the participating institution, some programs are delivered synchronously and some are delivered asynchronously. A list of participating universities along with courses of student and delivery method [can be found here](https://teachcyber.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/NCTAProgramDescriptionsNov2024.pdf).
