MS-ISAC is the Multi-State InformatSharing and Analysis Center, a division of the Center for Internet Security (CIS). MS-ISAC is tasked with improving the overall cybersecurity posture of state, local, tribal, and territorial governments through threat prevention, protection, response, and recovery (https://www.cisecurity.org/ms-isac/services)

MS-ISAC is a free resource of K-12 public schools offering the following services:

- 24/7 Security Operation Center
- Incident Response Services
- Cybersecurity Advisories and Notifications
- Access to Secure Portals for Communication and Document Sharing
- Cyber Alert Map
- Malicious Domain Blocking and Reporting (MDBR)
- Malicious Code Analysis Platform (MCAP)
- Weekly Top Malicious Domains/IP Report
- Monthly Members-only Webcasts
- Access to Cybersecurity Table-top Exercises
- Nationwide Cyber Security Review (NCSR)
- Awareness and Education Materials

In addition, MS-ISAC leverages relationships with Homeland Security to provide threat intelligence as part of it’s Homeland Security Information Network (HSIN), as well as access to DHS’s **[Cyber Hygiene Services](https://www.cisa.gov/cyber-hygiene-services), Cyber Resiliency Review via [via US-CERT](https://www.us-cert.gov/ccubedvp/sltt), and regional and national security exercises.**

Membership in the CIS SecureSuite is also included, which provides access to the CIS-CAT Pro Assessor, remediation content, and the CIS-CAT Pro Dashboard. You can **[Enroll in CIS SecureSuite Membership](https://enroll.cisecurity.org/) here.**

Additonal threat intelligence is available from MS-ISAC through a partnership with Deloitte’s Cyber Detect and Respond Portal. Registration for Deloitte threat intelligence is available from <https://cti.cisecurity.org>

Register for MS-ISAC at https://learn.cisecurity.org/ms-isac-registration.
