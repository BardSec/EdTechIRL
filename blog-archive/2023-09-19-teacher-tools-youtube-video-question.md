# Teacher Tools: YouTube Video Question Generator

*A Quick Way to Gain a Few Minutes Back in Your Day*

As an addition to [MagicSchool AI’s growing list of tools](https://www.edtechirl.com/p/ai-level-up-for-teachers), YouTube Video Question Generator is an awesome time saving tool for teachers.

To use it, first go to YouTube and copy the address from the video you’d like to generate questions for.

> NOTE: If the video owner didn’t enable captions, this feature won’t work.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Llln!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa24cd7a3-8ab4-4139-aa0d-2f372e133292_1071x796.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Llln!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa24cd7a3-8ab4-4139-aa0d-2f372e133292_1071x796.png)

Next, to access the Generator, login to [MagicSchool AI](https://app.magicschool.ai/) and select the YouTube Video Generator from the list of tools:

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!d3N_!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe4a2a7b0-7b0d-4e52-a46e-0f0ba717ccfd_1881x1077.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!d3N_!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe4a2a7b0-7b0d-4e52-a46e-0f0ba717ccfd_1881x1077.png)

In the Generator tool, select a grade level from K-College, number of questions (3, 5, 7, or 10), and question type (Multiple Choice, True False, or Free Response), insert the YouTube link into the Generator, and hit Generate.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Fx2P!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe430f7ec-2fe6-48a6-a2b5-e4c1aef7501e_907x532.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Fx2P!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe430f7ec-2fe6-48a6-a2b5-e4c1aef7501e_907x532.png)

In just a couple seconds, you’ll have a list of questions and answers based on the video.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!MH4K!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F4d020e05-9a2d-47b9-b36a-9c21382cc5f0_1238x361.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!MH4K!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F4d020e05-9a2d-47b9-b36a-9c21382cc5f0_1238x361.png)
