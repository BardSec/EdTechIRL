# Quick Tip: Making Timelines in PowerPoint

Assigning my students a digital timeline was always a nightmare, because it meant they would spend HOURS adjusting boxes and lines in PowerPoint. It’s a task that definitely lives at the “Substitution” phase of the [SAMR model](https://www.powerschool.com/blog/samr-model-a-practical-guide-for-k-12-classroom-technology-integration/#:~:text=The%20SAMR%20Model%20is%20a,Augmentation%2C%20Modification%2C%20and%20Redefinition.), so time spent doing it on a computer should either be faster than paper pencil or offer some other value to make it worthwhile. Nowadays, there’s a fast way to do it automagically in a format that can be easily shared or integrated into a larger presentation.

First, start with a fresh text box on your PPT slide and make a list of dates followed by events:

Highlight and right click on the dates and select “CONVERT TO SMARTART”
